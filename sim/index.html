What runs here: model/pe_model.py (the reference model the RTL is checked against cycle by cycle in
  test/test_core.py), asm/pe_asm.py and the device models of the model tests, unchanged,
  under Pyodide in a web worker. Not shown: the SPI bit timing of the host port, analog levels, the RTL itself.
  Times are clock cycles of an ideal clock and pins are logic levels (no pad delays).
